Add {{.Name}} to taskboard

Install the certificate for your device below, entering the passphrase chosen for it when asked. These downloads work for 10 minutes.

iPhone, iPad or Mac
Download profile
  1. iPhone or iPad: open Settings, tap Profile Downloaded, then Install, and enter the passphrase. Then turn on taskboard CA under General > About > Certificate Trust Settings.
  2. Mac: open System Settings > General > Device Management (Profiles on older macOS), double-click the profile, Install, and enter the passphrase.
Android
taskboard-ca.crt{{.P12}}
  1. In Settings, search for Install a certificate (under Encryption & credentials).
  2. Install taskboard-ca.crt as a CA certificate.
  3. Install {{.P12}} as a VPN & app user certificate, entering the passphrase. Use Chrome to open the board.
Windows
taskboard-ca.crt{{.P12}}
  1. Open taskboard-ca.crt, choose Install Certificate, Current User, and place it in Trusted Root Certification Authorities.
  2. Open {{.P12}}, choose Current User and enter the passphrase. Chrome and Edge use both.
Firefox, or Chrome on Linux
taskboard-ca.crt{{.P12}}
  1. Open certificate settings: in Firefox, Settings > Privacy & Security > View Certificates; in Chrome, Settings > Privacy and security > Security > Manage certificates.
  2. Under Authorities, import taskboard-ca.crt and trust it to identify websites.
  3. Under Your Certificates, import {{.P12}} and enter the passphrase.

Then quit and reopen the browser, and open the board.

If the board says this device has no certificate, the browser is still using its earlier choice: quit it completely and try again.
