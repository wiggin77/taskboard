Download profile
  1. Click Download profile.
  2. Open System Settings > General > Device Management (Profiles on older macOS) and double-click the taskboard profile. Click Install and enter the passphrase, then your Mac password if asked.
  3. Quit {{.BrowserName}} with ⌘Q, reopen it and open the board. When asked to use the certificate, choose Always Allow.

{{end}} {{template "pairfiles" .}}
  1. Tap both files to download them.
  2. Open the Settings app and search for Install a certificate (under Security, Encryption & credentials).
  3. Choose CA certificate, accept the warning and pick taskboard-ca.crt.
  4. Choose VPN & app user certificate, pick {{.P12}} and enter the passphrase.

{{- else if eq .Key "windows"}} {{template "pairfiles" .}}
  1. Download both files and open your Downloads folder.
  2. Open taskboard-ca.crt and click Install Certificate. Choose Current User, then Place all certificates in the following store, Browse, and Trusted Root Certification Authorities. Finish, and click Yes on the warning.
  3. Open {{.P12}}, choose Current User, enter the passphrase and keep the other defaults.
  4. Close every {{.BrowserName}} window, reopen it and open the board.
{{- else if eq .Key "chromeos"}} {{template "pairfiles" .}}
  1. Download both files.
  2. In Chrome, open Settings, search for certificates and open Manage certificates.
  3. Under Authorities, import taskboard-ca.crt and tick Trust this certificate for identifying websites.
  4. Under Your certificates, choose Import and Bind (or Import, if that isn't offered), pick {{.P12}} and enter the passphrase. Import and Bind keeps the key in the Chromebook's security chip.
  5. Close every Chrome window, reopen Chrome and open the board.
{{- else if eq .Key "linux"}} {{template "pairfiles" .}}
  1. Download both files.
  2. In {{.BrowserName}}, open Settings, search for certificates and open Manage certificates.
  3. Under Authorities, import taskboard-ca.crt and tick Trust this certificate for identifying websites.
  4. Under Your certificates, import {{.P12}} and enter the passphrase.
  5. Close every {{.BrowserName}} window, reopen it and open the board.
{{- else if eq .Key "firefox"}} {{template "pairfiles" .}}
  1. Download both files.
  2. In Firefox, open Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to Certificates and click View Certificates.
  3. On the Authorities tab, import taskboard-ca.crt and tick Trust this CA to identify websites.
  4. On the Your Certificates tab, import {{.P12}} and enter the passphrase.
  5. Quit Firefox, reopen it and open the board.
